Neill Lambert and Yi-Te Huang represented Quantum Toolbox in Python and Julia respectively at the 2026 RIKEN-NCHU Summer School in Taiwan in September.
They both gave tutorials demonstrating closed and open quantum system dynamics using [qutip (Python)](https://qutip.readthedocs.io/en/) and [QuantumToolbox.jl (Julia)](https://qutip.org/QuantumToolbox.jl). 

RIKEN-NCHU Summer School is a two-credit course in National Chung-Hsing University (Taiwan) from August 31 to September 2, 2026.
Six famous RIKEN research scientists leading in multidisciplinary research disciplines delivered lectures on their research fields over the course.
The lectures addressed different scientific subjects, including quantum physics, chemical physics, and ultrafast optics. 
More detail can be found in [2026 RIKEN-NCHU Summer School](https://sites.google.com/view/2026-riken-nchu-summer-school).
