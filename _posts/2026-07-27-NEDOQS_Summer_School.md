Yi-Te Huang represented Quantum Toolbox in Julia at the 2026 NEDOQS Summer School in Switzerland in July.
He gave tutorials demonstrating closed and open quantum system dynamics using [QuantumToolbox.jl (Julia)](https://qutip.org/QuantumToolbox.jl). 

Non-Equilibrium Dynamics of Open Quantum Systems (NEDOQS) is a one-week summer school on theory and simulation of open quantum systems held in Bernoulli Center, EPFL, Lausanne (Switzerland) from July 27 to 31, 2026.
This summer school offers a deep and modern theoretical introduction to open quantum dynamics.
Designed as an interdisciplinary bridge between quantum optics, many-body physics, and quantum information.
The program combines conceptual lectures on basic and advanced topics with hands-on computational tutorials. 
More detail can be found in [2026 NEDOQS Summer School](https://nedoqs2026.epfl.ch/).
