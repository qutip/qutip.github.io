World Wide Community

From Education to Industry

QuTiP is used to advance research in quantum optics, quantum information, quantum open systems, opto­mechanics, and many related fields. QuTiP is in use at nearly all research universities around the globe, government-funded research labs, and is relied upon by most major corporation developing a quantum computer.

Open-Source Community

QuTiP is used around the globe, and has been downloaded over a million times since its initial release. Need help in simulating a tricky problem? Our large community of users are happy to help in tackling even the most esoteric of issues.
